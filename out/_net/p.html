Help
Rankings Explained

Rankings are calculated based on results from league matches. Every player is given a starting rating, and this changes based on the results of their matches and the rating of their opponents.

About Your Rating

is the rating calculated at the end of the last completed period (monthly). is the most up-to-date rating calculated daily and is shown in bold.

Why am I not on the list?
  • You may not be registered as a member of this organisation
  • Your rating may be below the minimum threshold (0)
  • Your visibility setting may be set to hidden
How Ratings Are Calculated

When two players meet, the expected outcome is calculated based on the difference in their ratings. If you beat a higher-rated player, you gain more points. If you lose to a lower-rated player, you lose more points. The system ensures fair competition across all ability levels.

Periods Explained

Rankings are recalculated monthly. The Current rating is locked in at the end of each period. Between periods, the Provisional rating is updated daily as new results are entered.

Ratings are provided as a guide and may not reflect official rankings published by the national governing body.
