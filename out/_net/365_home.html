Our Software

League Manager

A complete league management system used by over 170 table tennis organisations across the UK. Designed for administrators who need reliable, easy-to-use tools to run their leagues.

Benefits

  • Reduces administrative workload with automated result processing
  • Provides real-time league tables, averages, and statistics
  • Accessible from any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile
  • Fully hosted and maintained — no software to install

Current Features

  • Automated league tables with 24 scoring systems and 17 ordering methods
  • Online match card submission with approval workflow
  • Individual and doubles player averages
  • Team and player statistics with form guides
  • Results charts (cross-tables)
  • Player of the Match tracking
  • Fixture scheduling with rearrangement support
  • Multi-division, multi-league support per organisation
  • Season archiving with full historical access

Availability

League Manager is available to all table tennis leagues and associations in the UK. To enquire about setting up your league, please contact us.

Membership Manager

A comprehensive membership system for counties, regions, and national governing bodies to manage registrations, renewals, and member records.

Benefits

  • Centralised member database with GDPR compliance
  • Online registration and renewal workflows
  • Club affiliation management
  • Privacy controls — members manage their own data visibility

Features

  • Member profiles with encrypted personal data
  • Subscription management with payment tracking
  • Role-based access for organisation administrators
  • Data export for reporting and compliance
  • Integration with League Manager for unified member records

Other Software

Additional modules available as bolt-ons to enhance your organisation's website:

  • Website Manager — CMS pages, news articles, photo galleries, menus
  • Ranking System — National and regional ranking calculations
  • Email Manager — Bulk email to members and contacts
  • Tournament Module — Tournament management and results entry
  • Banner Advertising — Sponsor banner management across multiple zones
  • Forum — Discussion forums for organisation members
